The challenge

Energy decisions shouldn't be complicated.

Unreliable electricity, generator costs and the upfront price of solar systems can make dependable power difficult to access. Finding trustworthy providers and understanding available options can be challenging too.

The proposed solution

A simpler way to explore clean energy

PowerWise Connect is planned as an ICT platform connecting users with clean-energy information, providers and financing opportunities.

01
⌕

Discover options

Explore clean-energy products and service providers relevant to your needs and location.

02
⇄

Compare and plan

Review system details, indicative costs and available payment or financing information.

03
✓

Connect and manage

Contact a provider and, in future releases, receive energy guidance and maintenance reminders.

Platform features

Provider directory

Find clean-energy providers and review their service information.

₦

Financing information

Understand available instalment and pay-as-you-go options offered by participating providers.

▥

Energy planning

Use simple guidance to think through electricity needs and consumption.

♧

Maintenance reminders

Receive optional reminders and service updates as the platform develops.

SMS

◎

Who it is for

Designed for everyday energy users

01Households

Families looking for more dependable and affordable energy options.

02Small businesses

Shops, salons, barbers, tailors and other businesses that rely on electricity.

03Underserved communities

People who face barriers to reliable electricity and clean-energy services.

04Clean-energy providers

Providers seeking to reach people interested in solar and other clean-energy solutions.

Our intended impact

More informed choices.
More access to clean energy.

PowerWise Connect aims to help users discover suitable energy options, understand financing choices and reduce avoidable energy costs. Impact will be assessed through user feedback, provider connections and clean-energy solutions accessed.

Development roadmap

Building in practical stages

  1. 01
    Understand user needs

    Speak with households and small businesses about their energy needs, budgets and challenges.

  2. 02
    Design the experience

    Prepare user flows, interface designs and the platform's technical structure.

  3. 03
    Build an initial website

    Develop provider listings, search, comparison and contact features.

  4. 04
    Test and improve

    Gather feedback from intended users and refine the platform before wider adoption.

  5. 05
    Expand access

    Explore USSD/SMS, alerts and energy-planning tools based on user feedback.
